# Assign crew through other schedule items

Crew rostering is an all-or-nothing feature: a crew assignment belongs to a crew need on an "other" schedule item, never to a whole project or a usage or billing item. A one-time migration automatically removes existing whole-project assignments and crew needs on usage or billing items, including their assignments, across all organisations rather than guessing how to migrate them; this can remove projects from affected people's "My projects". Reject changing an item with crew needs to usage or billing. When rostering is disabled, retain readable crew data but block crew changes, hide crew assignment functionality, and do not use those assignments to make projects appear in "My projects"; when enabled, assignments count regardless of their dates. Crew needs may still use custom time windows.

[ADR-0005](0005-crew-conflict-enforcement-and-reactivation.md) refines reactivation: retained assignments must pass activation checks before regaining active roster and "My projects" effects.
