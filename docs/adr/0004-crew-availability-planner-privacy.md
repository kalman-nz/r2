# Show unavailability without revealing its source

Planner availability views expose merged intervals clipped to the requested window, not the source of a block or another organisation's roster details; existing authorised access to the current organisation's roster is unchanged. This boundary applies to APIs, errors and exports, including normal administrator workflows.

Saving a clearly labelled public availability note shares its current text with authorised crew planners in all current organisations, including organisations joined later, without a separate publishing step. Clearing the note or ending membership stops future disclosure but cannot recall information already seen or exported. This deliberately favours simple organisation-wide sharing over per-organisation consent; the full disclosure, personal-view and revocation rules are recorded in [Define privacy-safe planner visibility and public notes](https://github.com/kalman-nz/r2/issues/16).
